Getting around Sokie

The four tabs, notifications, Emily and where to find everything.

Sokie has four tabs at the bottom of the screen.

  • Get Help: describe what you need and post an errand. This is where most people start.
  • Activity: every errand you've posted, live status and history.
  • Earn: run errands for other people and get paid. Apply here first.
  • More: payments, your crew, home care plans, invite & earn, settings and this Help Center.

Notifications

The bell at the top shows updates about your errands. You can also turn on push notifications so you hear about them right away. See Notifications.

Emily, your assistant

Tap the round Emily button to ask anything about Sokie, or describe a job and she'll help plan and price it.

You can replay the welcome tour any time: More → Help Center → Replay the tour.

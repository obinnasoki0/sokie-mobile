Become a runner

Apply in a few minutes from the Earn tab.

  1. Open the Earn tab and tap Get started.
  2. Fill in your legal name and address.
  3. Tell us how you get around and how far you'll travel.
  4. Verify your identity (see Get verified).
  5. Set up payouts (see Set up payouts).
  6. Accept the runner agreement and submit.

The Sokie team reviews your application. You'll be notified when you're approved, and then you'll start seeing jobs near you.

Runners are independent: you choose which jobs to take and when you work.

How to delete your account and data

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in at mysokie.com.
  2. Open More, then Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This permanently removes your profile, photos, documents and saved cards. Some records, such as payments, are kept as the law requires.

Or ask us

Email contact@mysokie.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete your data. We'll confirm when it's done.

Data from Facebook or Instagram

Sokie doesn't use Facebook or Instagram to sign people in. Sokie's own Facebook Page and Instagram account publish Sokie's posts; if you have questions about any data related to them, email contact@mysokie.com.

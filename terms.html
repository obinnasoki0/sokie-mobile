1. Who can use Sokie

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into a contract. Please provide accurate information and keep your account secure. You are responsible for activity on your account.

2. What Sokie is

Sokie is a marketplace that connects customers with independent workers and businesses ("Runners") for everyday errands and home and skilled services.

Sokie is a platform that makes introductions and handles booking and payment. It does not perform the services and does not employ Runners. The agreement to do the actual work is between you and the Runner.

3. How it works

You post a job, set a price or receive a quote or bids, a Runner is matched, the work is done, and you confirm it is complete in the app. For some jobs a Runner may suggest an add-on, which you can approve or decline.

4. Payments

Payments are handled securely by Stripe. When you book, your card is authorized (held) for the amount shown.

You are charged only after the job is completed and confirmed, and only up to the amount held. If the final amount is lower, we capture less and release the rest. The price is the Runner’s quote plus Sokie’s service fee, which uses declining rates on each Runner’s quote: 20% of the first $20, 15% of the next $30, 10% of the next $50 and 5% of anything above $100 (for example, a $200 quote has an $18.50 fee). Tips are optional and go entirely to the Runner. Fees are shown before you confirm. Jobs booked before October 11, 2026 keep the fees shown when they were booked.

Confirming completed work: when a Runner marks a job complete, you have 24 hours to confirm it. If you do not confirm within that time and there is no open dispute, the job is automatically confirmed and your card is charged for the work done (up to the amount held). This makes sure Runners are paid for work they have completed. If something is wrong, contact support or open a dispute before the 24 hours pass.

If something goes wrong, contact support and we will help resolve it. Refunds may be issued at our discretion and as required by law.

8b. Carpool

Carpool lets drivers share empty seats with riders going the same way, on a regular route or a one-time trip on chosen dates. Sokie suggests a fair seat price based on the driver’s running costs; drivers set their own price up to a cap, and riders may offer a different price, which the driver can accept, counter or decline. Sokie charges riders a booking fee of 10% of the seat price (minimum $0.75); drivers pay no fee.

Riders must be adults (18+). Riders meet the driver at the listed meeting point; drivers do not pick up at home addresses. Seats can be booked up to 60 days ahead. Your card is held before each trip (trips more than 6 days away are reserved on your saved card and held 6 days before) and charged after the trip. If a held or reserved seat can’t be held on your card, it is released.

Rider cancellations: free until 24 hours before departure. Between 24 and 2 hours before departure, 50% of the seat price (plus the booking fee) is charged; within 2 hours of departure, or if you don’t show up, the full seat price is charged. That amount goes to the driver for the seat they kept for you.

Driver cancellations: if a driver cancels a trip, stops a route or removes a rider, riders are released and never charged. A driver cancellation or removal within 24 hours of departure counts as a late cancellation (removals for safety reasons do not); three late cancellations within 60 days pauses the driver’s routes for review. Drivers may remove a rider and choose not to carry them again.

Waitlist: when a trip is full you can join its waitlist. If a seat opens, the first rider on the waitlist is booked automatically on their saved card and notified; the normal cancellation terms then apply. You can leave the waitlist at any time.

Carpool drivers must pass the same checks as Private Ride drivers (ID, background check, clean record, driver’s license, insurance and vehicle) and are responsible for insurance that covers their passengers.

Tech setup help at home

Boxes of cables and a manual nobody reads. Post your tech setup on Sokie and a vetted runner nearby gets it working.

Post an errandHow it works

What a runner can do for you

  • Setting up a new TV and streaming apps
  • Connecting a router, printer or smart speaker
  • Setting up a new phone, tablet or laptop
  • Everyday troubleshooting

In-home jobs need a runner with a clean record.

How it works

  1. Tell Emily what you need. In your own words, with photos if it helps.
  2. Choose your price. A fixed price, your own offer, or bids from runners.
  3. A vetted runner does it. ID verified, with a background check reviewed by the Sokie team.
  4. Confirm, then pay. Your card is held, not charged, until it's done and you confirm.

Part of Sokie's Technology Help service.

Questions

Will the runner see my passwords?

Only what you choose to share. You can enter passwords yourself.

Is this an IT repair service?

It's everyday setup and troubleshooting, not repairs to the hardware itself.

When am I charged?

Only after the job is done and you confirm.

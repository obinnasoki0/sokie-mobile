Someone to run your errands

Can't be in two places at once? Tell Emily what you need in your own words and a vetted runner nearby handles it.

Post an errandHow it works

What a runner can do for you

  • A list of small errands in one trip
  • Personal and admin tasks
  • Waiting in for a delivery
  • Anything legal, safe and reasonable

Describe it like you'd text a friend: Emily turns it into a clear job and suggests a fair price.

How it works

  1. Tell Emily what you need. In your own words, with photos if it helps.
  2. Choose your price. A fixed price, your own offer, or bids from runners.
  3. A vetted runner does it. ID verified, with a background check reviewed by the Sokie team.
  4. Confirm, then pay. Your card is held, not charged, until it's done and you confirm.

Part of Sokie's Personal & Administrative Help service.

Questions

What can I ask for?

Everyday errands that are legal, safe and reasonable. Emily will ask a question if something needs clarifying.

How do I know it's done?

Runners take photos as the work happens, and you confirm before you're charged.

Is it free to cancel?

Free before a runner is assigned. Later cancellations may carry a fee, shown before you confirm.

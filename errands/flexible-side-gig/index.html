A flexible side gig, on your terms

Evenings, weekends, between other work: run everyday errands for people nearby when it suits you.

Apply to runHow earning works

Jobs you can pick up

  • Choose jobs by type, distance and pay
  • Counter-offer if a price looks low
  • Build regular customers who book you again
  • Level up by adding licences and certificates

Runners are independent: you choose which jobs to take and when you work.

How it works

  1. Apply in minutes. Sign up and open the Earn tab.
  2. Get verified. ID check, a background check reviewed by the Sokie team, and payouts set up.
  3. Pick your jobs. See your exact take-home before you accept, or counter-offer.
  4. Get paid. Payouts after the customer confirms, and 100% of tips.

Questions

Is there a minimum number of hours?

No. You choose which jobs to take and when.

Can I see the pay before I accept?

Yes, your exact take-home is shown before you accept.

How do I apply?

Sign up and open the Earn tab. The Sokie team reviews your application.

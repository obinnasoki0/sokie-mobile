Grocery pickup and delivery

Out of milk, stuck at work, or just not up for the store? Post your grocery list on Sokie and a vetted runner nearby shops it and brings it to you.

Post an errandHow it works

What a runner can do for you

  • A weekly grocery shop from your list
  • A quick top-up of a few things you ran out of
  • Picking up an order you already paid for at the store
  • Household basics from your list

You set a purchase budget: the most the runner may spend on items. You're charged for what's actually bought, plus the job, and the rest of the hold is released.

The runner sends receipts, and you approve the spend before it counts.

How it works

  1. Tell Emily what you need. In your own words, with photos if it helps.
  2. Choose your price. A fixed price, your own offer, or bids from runners.
  3. A vetted runner does it. ID verified, with a background check reviewed by the Sokie team.
  4. Confirm, then pay. Your card is held, not charged, until it's done and you confirm.

Part of Sokie's Shopping & Store Runs service.

Questions

How do I pay for the groceries?

Your card is held for the job plus your purchase budget. After the job is done and you confirm, you're charged only for the items actually bought plus the job; the rest is released.

What if something on my list isn't available?

Message your runner in the app while they shop. They can suggest a swap and you decide.

Can I tip the runner?

Yes. Tips are optional and go 100% to the runner.

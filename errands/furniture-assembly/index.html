Furniture assembly help

The box has been leaning against the wall for weeks. Post it on Sokie and a vetted runner nearby puts it together for you.

Post an errandHow it works

What a runner can do for you

  • Flat-pack bookshelves, desks and wardrobes
  • Bed frames and cribs
  • Shelving and storage units
  • Outdoor furniture

Add a photo of the box or the item so the runner knows what they're building and what to bring.

Runners photograph the finished job, so you can check it before you confirm.

How it works

  1. Tell Emily what you need. In your own words, with photos if it helps.
  2. Choose your price. A fixed price, your own offer, or bids from runners.
  3. A vetted runner does it. ID verified, with a background check reviewed by the Sokie team.
  4. Confirm, then pay. Your card is held, not charged, until it's done and you confirm.

Part of Sokie's Assembly & Setup service.

Questions

Do I need to provide tools?

Say in the job what tools you have. Emily can list what the runner should bring.

How is the price set?

A fixed price, your own offer, or bids. Emily suggests a fair price from what you describe.

What if it isn't done right?

Don't confirm: open the job and tap Report a problem. The Sokie team reviews it.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · Beta release

Our promise

We keep this simple: here is what we collect, why, and who we share it with. This applies to the Sokie beta in the United States.

1. Information we collect

  • Account & profile: your name, email, phone, password, and addresses.
  • Jobs: task details, locations, scheduling, messages, photos, and reviews you provide.
  • Location: your device location to match and route jobs, and a Runner’s live location while a job is active.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We receive limited details (like card type and last four digits) and do not store full card numbers.
  • Runner verification: for Runners, identity verification through Stripe Identity (which may include an ID and selfie, handled by Stripe), and any license, insurance, or background information where used.
  • Device & usage: basic app and device information to keep Sokie working and safe.

2. How we use it

To run the service and match jobs; process payments and payouts; verify identity and keep the community safe; provide support and send service updates and reminders; and improve Sokie. We send marketing only where allowed and you can opt out anytime.

3. Who we share it with

  • The other person on a job (customer and Runner), so the job can be done — for example name, task, location, and live tracking.
  • Service providers that help us operate: Stripe (payments & identity), maps/geocoding, notifications, and secure cloud hosting. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Legal & safety reasons, when required by law or to protect people.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where your data is handled

Sokie currently operates in the United States, and your data is processed in the U.S. by us and our service providers.

5. Your choices & rights

You can access, update, or delete your information, and manage notifications in the app or by contacting us. Depending on your U.S. state (for example, California), you may have additional privacy rights — just reach out and we will help. Email contact@mysokie.com.

6. Security & retention

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and access controls. No system is perfectly secure. We keep information only as long as needed to run the service and meet legal and business requirements.

7. Children

Sokie is for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes & contact

We may update this policy and will post the new version in the app. Questions or requests: contact@mysokie.com. (Sokie)

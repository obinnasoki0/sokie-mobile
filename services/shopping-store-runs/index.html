Shopping & Store Runs

Purchase approved items and deliver them to the customer. Book it on Sokie and a vetted runner nearby does it. You pay only once it's done and you confirm.

Post an errandEarn as a runner

You set a budget for the most the runner may spend on items, and you're charged only for what's actually bought, plus the job.

How it works

  1. Tell Emily what you need. Describe the job in your own words, with photos if it helps.
  2. Choose your price. A fixed price, your own offer, or bids from runners. Emily suggests a fair price.
  3. A vetted runner does it. Every runner is ID-verified, with a background check reviewed by the Sokie team.
  4. Confirm, then pay. Your card is held, not charged, until the job is done and you confirm.

Questions

When am I charged?

Booking places a hold on your card, not a charge. You're charged only after the job is done and you confirm it, for the work actually done; the rest is released.

How are runners vetted?

Every runner verifies their identity with a government ID and a live selfie, has a background check reviewed by the Sokie team, and is approved by the team before they can see a job.

How do I set the price?

Choose a fixed price, make your own offer, or let runners bid. Emily, Sokie's assistant, suggests a fair price; runners decide whether to take the job.

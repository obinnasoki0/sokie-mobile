For Runners

Thanks for helping test Sokie. Here is how working through Sokie works during the beta. These are in addition to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

1. You are an independent contractor

You run your own independent business. You are not an employee of Sokie. You choose which jobs to accept, when to work, and how to do the work, and you can work elsewhere. You are responsible for your own taxes, equipment, and expenses.

1a. No subcontracting

You must personally perform the jobs you accept. Subcontracting, delegating, or sending someone else in your place is not allowed, and you may not share, sell, or transfer your Sokie account. The person we verify is the person who does the work (for a business account, the verified business and its own vetted workers). Violations can result in removal from the platform.

2. Getting started

You must be 18+, legally allowed to do the work, and complete onboarding, which may include identity verification (via Stripe Identity) and, where it applies, background or credential checks. Keep your information accurate.

3. Licenses, insurance & verification

You are responsible for holding any licenses, permits, and insurance the law requires for the work you offer — especially for skilled trades. Do not accept jobs you are not qualified or permitted to do. Please provide proof when asked.

Both individuals and businesses may offer licensed trades (e.g. plumbing, electrical, HVAC), but you must upload your license or trade certificate and be verified first. Until then, you will not see or be matched to licensed-trade jobs. The verified individual or business must personally perform the work — no subcontracting.

3a. Business teams

A business account may add the people who do its jobs as team members. Each team member must have their own Sokie account, pass Sokie's identity and background checks, and be confirmed by the business before taking jobs; only team members who have done so may work on Sokie jobs for the business. Team members see and accept jobs themselves, only in categories the business offers, and only while the business remains approved, verified and insured.

Pay for a team member's jobs (including tips and any compensation) goes to the business's payout account, not to the team member. The business is solely responsible for paying its team members, for their wages, taxes and benefits, and for their conduct on jobs. Team members can leave, and businesses can remove them, at any time; jobs already accepted are still paid to the business.

4. How you get paid

For each completed job you receive your accepted quote minus Sokie’s commission: 15% for most jobs, or 20% in a few categories, shown on the job. The commission applies only to job pay — never to tips (which are 100% yours) or to purchases you’re reimbursed for — and you always see your exact payout before you accept. Customers pay their own separate service fee. If a customer cancels after you’ve set out and a cancellation fee applies, 75% of it is paid to you. Jobs posted before October 11, 2026 keep their original terms. Payouts are made through Stripe Connect — you will set up a Stripe account and agree to Stripe’s Connected Account terms. The customer’s payment is held and released on completion; payout timing follows Stripe’s schedule. Tips go to you.

After you mark a job complete, the customer has 24 hours to confirm it. If they do not confirm within that time and there is no open dispute, the job is automatically confirmed so you are paid for the work you completed.

5. Taxes

You are responsible for reporting and paying your own taxes on what you earn. Sokie and/or Stripe may provide tax forms where required and may need to report certain information.

6. Doing the work

Do the job professionally, safely, and lawfully; bring your own tools and transport unless agreed otherwise; communicate through the app; and get the customer’s approval before doing extra work or changing the price.

Never carry illegal, stolen or hazardous items (illegal drugs, firearms, ammunition, explosives, fireworks, hazardous chemicals). Only transport dogs and cats. Do not pick up, transport, watch or wait with children or minors. Carry adult passengers only on a Private Ride job or a Carpool route or trip you posted (where you may charge no more than the seat-price cap), and only once Sokie has verified your driver's license, insurance and vehicle (in addition to your ID and background check); you are responsible for holding insurance that covers carrying paying passengers. Sokie jobs are tasks only: do not accept or offer company, hanging out, dates or visits without a task. Before carrying anything, check what it is — it must be open for you to see inside before it is sealed, unless it is still sealed by the store, manufacturer or a carrier; never accept cash, gift cards or closed envelopes. If a job turns out to involve anything prohibited, stop and use "Report this job" in the app — you will not be penalized for refusing.

6b. Checking packages is your responsibility

Before you take custody of anything for a job, you must personally check it: open it and look inside (or confirm it is still sealed by the store, manufacturer or a carrier), take the photos the app asks for, and refuse it if you cannot see what it is or believe it may be illegal, stolen, hazardous or otherwise prohibited.

Sokie's automatic photo check is only an aid. It does not inspect, verify or approve what you carry, and an item passing it does not make the item lawful or move responsibility for it to Sokie.

You alone are responsible for what you accept and carry and, to the fullest extent the law allows, for any legal consequences that arise from it, including claims, fines, penalties and costs. You agree to indemnify Sokie against them where they result from your failure to check an item properly.

You can always refuse an item at pickup, with no penalty.

7. Keep it on Sokie

For jobs you get through Sokie, do not arrange payment off-platform to avoid fees or divert Sokie customers away from the app.

8. Customer information

Use customer information only to do the job, keep it private, and do not reuse it for your own purposes.

9. Ratings & account

Your profile may show ratings and verification status. We may suspend or deactivate accounts for safety concerns, policy or legal violations, or loss of required credentials.

10. Beta disclaimer, liability & ending

Sokie is provided "as is" during beta. To the extent the law allows, Sokie is not liable for indirect losses, and our responsibility to you is limited to the commissions we earned from your recent jobs. You can stop anytime, and we may end the beta or your access as described here.
